% \begin{abstract}
	This book presents the mathematical and numerical foundations of modern data science.
	% 
	It covers signal and image processing (Fourier analysis, wavelets, denoising, and compression), imaging science (inverse problems, sparsity, and compressed sensing), and machine learning (regression, classification, and deep learning).
	%
	The chapters develop the underlying tools---linear operators, nonlinear approximation, probability, and convex optimization---and show how these tools lead to efficient algorithms.
	%
	The book also serves as a theoretical companion to the Numerical Tours\footnote{\url{https://www.numerical-tours.com}}, which provide practical implementations in MATLAB, Python, Julia, and R.
		
% \end{abstract}
