# Artwork by Louis Peyré

These images are details from works by **Louis Peyré (1923–2012)**, supplied by
Gabriel Peyré for the Mathematical Tours website and its book covers.
The original JPEG files are preserved without changes. The filenames describe
subjects or groups of works; they are not asserted to be catalogue titles.

[Discover the artist’s work](https://www.louispeyre.com/). The homepage includes
his self-portrait and an explanation of the images’ provenance. Visible banner
and cover credits link to the artist’s website.

Banner selections and alternative text live in `_data/artworks.json`. Each book
keeps a copy of its cover image so that its sources remain independently
buildable; its cover-credit record identifies this original and its SHA-256 hash.
The resource and slide previews are examples of the teaching material, whose
sources are recorded in `docs/visual-assets.json`.
